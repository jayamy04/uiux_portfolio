Overview

Bloom & Vine is a Davis wedding florist with beautiful work but a weak web presence — past weddings were buried and booking paths were unclear.

I redesigned the site with occasion-based galleries, photo carousels, and prominent contact CTAs so couples could browse the portfolio and reach out quickly.
