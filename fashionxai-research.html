FashionXAI Research

Goal
understand pain points around purchasing clothing online when it comes to size and fit
Methods
Netnography and Interviews

FashionXAI app

Netnography Research Questions

  1. 01

    What problems do people face when trying to purchase clothing online? (having to do with sizing and fit of clothing)

  2. 02

    What workarounds do people have for dealing with these problems?

3 Problems

  1. 1

    Lack of Industry-Wide Measurement Standards - what exactly is a sleeve length? How do I measure sleeve length?

  2. 2

    Fit Is Subjective - One person's true to size is another persons' baggy

  3. 3

    Technical Limitations - It's just hard to do at scale ~ time consuming/expensive; color can be decieving

Findings

Quality/fit uncertainty online

Quality/fit uncertainty online. “I buy brands I trust or if I'm taking a risk brands that can be returned easily” “You can never be 100% sure, but reviews and sizing charts help. I also won't ever buy online if the return policy is bad, just in case.”

Taking measurements, size guides, preferred silhouettes (coffeeplease1972)

Taking measurements, size guides, preferred silhouettes (coffeeplease1972). “Your body measurements only tell you the smallest size you can fit into, not the ideal size of clothing you should wear” (kinaandtam TikTok). Compare with clothes you already own.

Size charts don't always have enough information (Mary D)

Size charts don't always have enough information (Mary D). “size charts unfortunately often just take the average measurements for that size/brand rather than being unique to each item” (Ghost Friend Clothing). Size charts put 1-2 sizes larger than in person (fizzlepop). Generic size charts don't account for fit preferences (RoboAly).

Inconsistent sizing across brands

Inconsistent sizing across brands. Labeling “high rise” vs inches. Not offering technical specifications when ordering online. Plus/straight size gap. Not stocking full range in brick-and-mortar. Inaccurate representation (not on person, computer-generated). Quality going down, can't see in person.

Workarounds: measurements, compare with owned clothes, reviews, order 3 sizes

Workarounds: measurements, compare with owned clothes, reviews, order 3 sizes. Good return policy + detailed garment measurements. Try in store then buy online.

Can't try on online, returns wasteful

Can't try on online, returns wasteful. Petite sizing issues. out of 200ish items, ~30 needed alteration, ~40 unwearable (Peregrinebullet).
