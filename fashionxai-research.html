Size and Fit Netnography and Interviews

Goal
understand pain points around purchasing clothing online when it comes to size and fit
Methods
Netnography and Interviews

3 Problems

  1. 1 Lack of Industry-Wide Measurement Standards — what exactly is a sleeve length? How do I measure sleeve length?
  2. 2 Fit Is Subjective — One person’s true to size is another persons’ baggy
  3. 3 Technical Limitations — It’s just hard to do at scale ~ time consuming/expensive; color can be decieving

Netnography

Research Questions:

  1. 01

    What problems do people face when trying to purchase clothing online? (having to do with sizing and fit of clothing)

  2. 02

    What workarounds do people have for dealing with these problems?

Participants/Sources:

  • Reddit threads
  • Article/blog comments
  • TikTok comments

Findings

Lack of Industry-Wide Measurement Standards

Online size charts don’t always give all the measurements needed. Size charts aren’t always accurate. misleading titles. Not offering the technical specifications when ordering online.

Fit Is Subjective

inconsistent sizing across items/brands. [problem] Generic size charts may not account for individual fit preferences, leading to ill-fitting purchases. ACTUAL PROBLEM WITH BUYING ONLINE, EVEN WITH MEASUREMENTS

Technical Limitations

inaccurate representation. inaccurate representations. painpoint in returns/ inaccurate representations. How ironic that as the quality of items is going way down we aren’t able to see them in person to buy anymore 😭

Quality and fit uncertainty online

Doesn’t know if clothes will be good quality/ fit right when shopping online. Online shopping is not easy bc you can’t try it on. Wasteful when returned. Some stats: 30 needed alterations, 40 were unwearable out of 200

Workarounds

Doesn’t risk online shopping unless they know the brand or there’s a good return policy. Possible selling point of our product, increase sales, minimize returns. Sticks to brands they trust. Take personal measurements, and compare w size chart. Consistently takes measurements and checks with stores sizing chart. Good return policy + detailed measurements by seller. Try in store. Free shipping + return
