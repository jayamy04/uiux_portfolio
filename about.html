About

me

I've done UX research and design at Nordstrom Rack, IBM, and Sound Transit, spanning consumer behavior research, enterprise dashboard redesign, and information architecture overhaul.

I'm a published researcher at UIST 2023, focused on making sports more accessible to diverse communities, including people with disabilities. I've also partnered with Inclusive Lab on making public art more accessible.

Beyond my main work, I love growing communities and supporting startups. I founded the Cognitive Science Student Association (now 900+ members) with its first annual conference drawing 150+ participants. I also led branding for WiCS (+300 members), helped SacHacks grow to 500+ participants as Sacramento's biggest hackathon, and supported the launch of SchedGo, $20K First Place Big Bang! Prize.

Outside of work, I dance, run, and read.
