In-person Shopping Experiences Survey (Nordstrom Rack)

Role
UX Researcher · Lead
Client
Nordstrom Rack
Methods
Survey · User interviews (in progress)
Nordstromrack.com ↗

Overview

In collaboration with Nordstrom Rack, I led an independent research on how to improve the in-person shopping experience for shoppers unsure of their personal style. I wanted to learn how they define their personal style and how shopping can better support and develop a better one.

Goals

  1. Goal 1

    To evaluate the flow, satisfaction, pain points, and areas for improvement of the in-store shopping experience for people who are unsure about their personal style

  2. Goal 2

    To explore if they use any tools, apps, or services would help them shop or discover their personal style

Research questions

  1. 01

    What is a typical shopping experience like for people who are not confident in their personal style?

  2. 02

    How do people define their personal style?

  3. 03

    What does personal style mean to these shoppers?

  4. 04

    What makes shopping a good or bad experience for them?

  5. 05

    What have they tried to find in their personal style?

  6. 06

    Do shoppers want to discover their own personal style?

  7. 07

    Do shoppers want in-person shopping to improve?

  8. 08

    Is there anything that shoppers are currently doing to improve their shopping experience or find their personal style?

Target User Group(s)

Including criteria

  1. 1 People who shop at least three times a year
  2. 2 People who have or had difficulty finding their personal style
  3. 3 Diverse demographics (age, gender identity, race and ethnicity)

Excluding criteria

  1. 1 People who have never struggled with shopping or have never shopped for themselves or others
  2. 2 People who live or usually shop outside the US
  3. 3 People who are researchers or work in retail or fashion to prevent potential biases

Surveys

I chose to conduct surveys because they can help gather information quickly from various people. I hope to find new pain points and gaps in the current shopping experiences and personal style discovery.

User Interviews (in progress)

User Interviews can help me understand people’s experiences in detail. I plan to conduct interviews with both survey respondents and others who are interested in participating.

Interview Content

Introduction

Hi, and thank you for participating in the interview. My name is Amy, and I am a second year Master’s student in Human Computer Design and Engineering. This project is a student-led project in collaboration with Nordstrom Rack about shopping and personal style discovery and I will be guiding you through today’s session. Before I start, I would like you to read over and submit an electronic consent form. I’ll send you the consent form now over chat. Please take your time reading over it and let me know if you have any questions. Once you’ve submitted the form, please let me know!

The goal of today’s session is for me to learn about your experience shopping in-person and how you think about your personal style. I’ll ask a few questions about both throughout the interview and you are allowed to skip any questions you are not comfortable answering.

I’ll be taking notes while we talk, but I’ll mostly be listening.

Some things to keep in mind:

  • This is not an examination
  • You are not being tested, I am here to learn from your shopping experience and personal style. There are no right or wrong answers.
  • During this study, please share as much detail as you feel comfortable with. Small details often help us understand your experience better.
  • Everything you are thinking is important, and I’m interested in any comments, both positive and negative, that you can provide. Do you have any questions before we get started?

This interview contains two main contents, first I will ask about your shopping experience and then personal style discovery.

During the interview, I would like to record the meeting so that I can review them later. All responses will be used for research purposes only and no data will be linked to your name. Is this ok with you?

Do you have any questions before we get started?

Questions

  • How important is shopping to you?
  • Can you describe what a typical in-person shopping experience looks like for you?
  • What do you find easiest about in-person shopping, if any?
  • What do you find most difficult about in-person shopping, if any?
  • Have you used, if any, tools, apps, or services to help you shop in-person? If yes, can you describe how you used them?
  • What aspects were helpful or unhelpful to you?
  • What would you find valuable or helpful from these tools/apps/services?
  • How important is personal style discovery to you?
  • Can you briefly define what personal style means to you?
  • Would you say you found your personal style?
  • What could make personal style discovery easier for you, if any?
  • What difficulties, if any, do you experience when trying to find your personal style?
  • Have you done, or are you doing anything currently to help you find your personal style?
  • What aspects were helpful or unhelpful to you?
  • That was the last question I have prepared for you. Is there anything I did not ask about that you think I should know?

Post-Interview Questions

That concludes the interview. Before we end the session, I have two concluding questions for you.

  • Would you be interested in being contacted about future research opportunities?
  • Is there anyone who would be interested in participating in this study? If so, please let me know so I can reach out to them. No worries if not.

If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to ask now or reach out at slee45@uw.edu after the session.

Thank you for participating in our interview! We truly appreciate your time and feedback. This concludes the session.
