Projects

Nordstrom Rack “Buy Online & Pick Up in Store” Research

Evaluative Research

Angela, a Principal UX researcher at Nordstrom, spotted the same- or next-day pickup filter failing—and it had never been tested with real shoppers. I helped define the parameters, recruit participants, facilitate sessions, and report findings to her UX team. Across 8 remote usability tests with shoppers near Nordstrom Rack stores, one question drove the work: how easily can users navigate the desktop site to find and purchase same- or next-day pickup items?

About

Amy has worked at Nordstrom Rack (improving the online pickup experience), IBM (leading discovery and empathy workshops), and Sound Transit (redesigning their website).

She is also a published researcher at UIST 2023 (AR/VR accessibility in sports) and currently working on AI accessibility in public spaces and art.

Beyond her main work, she builds communities from the ground up like the Cognitive Science Student Association (900+ members) and helps startups like SchedGo (AI scheduling tool) grow.
