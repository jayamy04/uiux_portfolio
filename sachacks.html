Overview

SacHacks is Sacramento's largest hackathon — but when I joined, it had no brand, website, or social presence. Organizers couldn't register participants or reach sponsors.

I designed the full digital presence from zero: brand identity, responsive website, and Instagram campaigns that helped grow attendance to 500+ participants from across Northern California.
