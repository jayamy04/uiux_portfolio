RuleMate App

RuleMate is an interactive AR/VR mobile experience designed to help beginner players learn board games more quickly. Learning a new board game is often frustrating—rulebooks are long, hard to follow, and lack engagement. Many players lose motivation before even starting, limiting their willingness to explore new games. RuleMate reimagines how players learn by transforming static instructions into an interactive, visual, and guided experience. By combining mobile interaction with AR-enhanced learning, the app helps users understand rules in context, practice gameplay, and ask questions in real time.

[ Key Features ]

Design Solution

RuleMate removes the pressure of having to have an expert in the room and allows all players to:

  • Learn the setup and rules of the game
  • Play a practice round with everyone
  • Ask clarifying questions to an AI assistant GameBot
  • Become proficient in the gameplay and rules
[ Conclusion ]

Reflection

By translating complex, text-heavy rulebooks into intuitive and interactive experiences, the design aims to reduce confusion and improve confidence for beginner players. Emphasizing clear structure, visual guidance, and real-time support, RuleMate creates a more accessible and engaging way to learn and play.
