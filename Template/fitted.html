FITTED App

FITTED aims to make online clothing shopping more accurate and confidence-driven by leveraging personalized body measurements to predict how a garment will fit. Users begin by entering their measurements, selecting fit preferences for specific stores, and uploading receipts from past purchases. They can also create personal moodboards to reflect their evolving style. Once onboarded, they are directed to a homepage where they can browse clothing tailored to their fit profile and receive style recommendations based on their preferences and past shopping behaviors.

  • [ UI/UX Designer ] Michael Bui, Amy Lee, Christina Marquez, Ko Motonaga
  • [ Year ] 2025
  • [ Status ] Completed
[ Key Features ]

Solution

FITTED personalizes the shopping experience by combining body measurements, fit preferences, and past purchases to predict how clothing will fit.
