Overview

Degree planning across registrar sites is slow, confusing, and error-prone. SchedGo is an AI-powered web app that helps college students plan their degrees and build class schedules without conflicts.

I led UX and front-end engineering — from profile onboarding and course search through to a weekly calendar that displays every valid schedule combination the AI generates.

The product scaled to 500+ active users across four California colleges and won $20K First Place and People's Choice at UC Davis Big Bang!

  • Conflict-free schedule generation
  • Profile onboarding (school, major, planning context)
  • Smart course search with conflict-aware filters
  • Weekly calendar view of generated schedules
