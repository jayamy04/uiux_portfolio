Overview

IBM Client Engineering brings together interdisciplinary teams — Business Technology Leader, Solution Architect, Data Scientist, Technology Engineer, and Designer — to work with enterprise clients before a solution is chosen. My internship spanned three projects: conducting and creating Mural workshop sessions with CVS Health and MCNA (UnitedHealth Group), redesigning the IBM SaaS Console notification system with Brian Calder and Rami Alayan, and creating an MVP resource guide for future designer interns.

Design project — Mural workshop sessions with CVS Health and MCNA (UHG)

Conducting and/or creating Mural workshop sessions with CVSH and MCNA (UHG) in discovery. Led by Mark Ainscow, Innovation Designer.

What was the issue?

CVSH: Overreliance on on-prem infrastructure.

UHG: Confident in their solutioning, but not discovery.

What did we do?

CVSH: Discovery session (readiness assessment) & co-create the data migration plan.

UHG: Discovery session (what are the issues you are facing?).

What I learned

Being able to bring people with the right expertise is crucial.

Migration process

  1. Assessing Readiness
  2. Preparing Source FileNet Content Manager for the move
  3. Prepare target environment for FileNet content manager
  4. Installing the operator
  5. Deploy Containers
  6. Complete Post Deployment Tasks

Readiness assessment

  • Software Product and Compatibility Report
  • Existing architecture
  • High Availability Disaster Recovery
  • Custom Step Processors
  • Using CPE Virtual Member Manager directory configuration
  • Storage and existing data
  • Custom applications using the CPE EJB protocol
  • External Data Service

Discovery questions

  • What problem are you solving? Hierarchy of problems
  • How are you approaching the problem today (current state)
  • Metrics around the problems
  • Who are the users?
  • What is the client's definition of success?
  • What is the high level process (the as-is)
  • Does the client have a high level workflow/sketch diagram they are going from?
  • Does the solution scale up to other areas?
  • What is the compelling reason to act?
  • What are the importance of the dates?
  • What is the best fit solution to deliver your concerns?
  • Is it really Watson Orchestrate/ RPA?

Software project — SaaS Console notification redesign

SaaS Console Notification Redesign with Brian Calder (Lead UX Designer) and Rami Alayan (Design Manager).

What issue did we address?

Customers are not able to see when the trial or subscription has ended or has a problem.

What value does this hold for me & IBM?

I am interested in UX design & I helped the project move more smoothly and faster.

How did we solve it?

We created a dashboard with notifications regarding trials or subscriptions. I also created the notification content.
